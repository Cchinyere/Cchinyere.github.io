About Me

I bring a blend of of hands-on security operations, cyber governance, assurance and responsible AI expertise, helping organisations move from reactive security activity to clearer risk decisions, stronger controls and trusted digital adoption.

Professional Profile

I am a cybersecurity professional with experience across security operations, governance, risk and compliance, IT service management, business process improvement, security awareness and AI governance. My work focuses on helping organisations investigate threats, understand risk, strengthen controls and make clear, evidence-based security decisions.

In security operations, I support alert triage, investigation and incident response using Microsoft Defender XDR, Microsoft Sentinel and KQL. I have also contributed to data loss prevention and security awareness initiatives, including work that helped reduce potential customer data breach risk.

Professional Strengths

  • Security operations experience across alert triage, investigation, escalation and incident response.
  • GRC capability across cyber risk assessment, ISO 27001, control review, policy documentation and assurance reporting.
  • AI governance focus covering responsible AI, ISO/IEC 42001, AI risk assessment and secure AI adoption.
  • Ability to translate technical findings into clear business language for stakeholders and decision-makers.
  • Research background in web application security, penetration testing and OWASP methodology, with work presented at ACROSET 2024 and accepted for IEEE Xplore publication.

Professional Direction

My current focus is on practical, measurable security improvement. I am particularly interested in how organisations can move beyond tick-box compliance by using better risk visibility, stronger awareness, clearer control ownership and governance practices that support secure adoption of emerging technologies.

I bring a multidisciplinary perspective shaped by cybersecurity operations, AI governance, cybersecurity advisory, financial services technology, IT service management, process improvement, academic research and community contribution. This allows me to approach cybersecurity as both a technical discipline and a business resilience priority.

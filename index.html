Professional Profile

My work sits where security monitoring, risk, governance, and business decisions meet. I have hands-on experience supporting alert triage, investigation, escalation, and incident response using Microsoft Defender XDR, Microsoft Sentinel, and KQL. I also bring a strong governance lens through cyber risk assessment, control review, policy documentation, security awareness, and AI governance work.

What makes my profile different is the range of environments I have worked across. I have supported security operations, IT service management, business process improvement, financial services technology, awareness initiatives, research, and practical framework development. That gives me a rounded view of cybersecurity: not just what alerts show, but what organizations need to do next, who needs to own the action, and how the control should work in practice.
